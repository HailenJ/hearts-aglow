Privacy

Hearts Aglow · last updated 10 November 2026

heartsaglow.io

The site sets no cookies and has no accounts. Page views and clicks are counted with GoatCounter, which does not use cookies or track you across sites. Music plays through Bandcamp's player, the OTO trailer loads from YouTube's privacy-enhanced player only when you press play, and the newsletter signup opens beehiiv. Each of those services has its own privacy policy.

Hearts Aglow Stats

Hearts Aglow Stats is a private dashboard used only by Hailen Jackson to see statistics for Hearts Aglow's own accounts. When it is connected to YouTube, it reads view, watch-time and subscriber statistics for the Hearts Aglow channel through the YouTube Analytics and YouTube Data APIs. It does not read anyone else's data, post anything, or change anything on YouTube.

  • The statistics are stored in a private database that only Hailen can open, and are never sold, shared or used for advertising.
  • Hearts Aglow Stats' use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
  • Access can be revoked at any time from your Google account permissions; stored statistics can be deleted on request.
